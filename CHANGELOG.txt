CHANGELOG

## 1.3.0 - 2026-07-31

- Pausing mid-session (during an exercise or the rest gap) now shows a
  full-screen "Paused" state with a big pause icon and a Resume button,
  instead of just freezing the timer in place.
- Each 30-second exercise now ends with a short beep, so you don't need to
  be looking at the phone to know time's up.
- The "Last 4 weeks" view now shows the number of exercises completed (the
  level at the time) behind the tick on each completed day, e.g. "2".
  GET /api/history now returns `{ completed: [{ date, level }, ...] }`
  instead of a plain date array.

## 1.2.1 - 2026-07-21

- Fixed the "Last 4 weeks" view showing fewer than 28 days and greyed-out
  future dates whenever today wasn't a Sunday. It now always covers a true
  trailing 28-day window ending today (partial first week, full weeks in
  between, partial current week), with blank leading cells to keep the grid
  aligned under the Mon-Sun weekday header.

## 1.2.0 - 2026-07-19

- Added a "Last 4 weeks" view (burger menu → Last 4 weeks): a full-screen,
  at-a-glance calendar of the last 28 days as a weekday-aligned 4x7 grid.
  Completed days show a green tick, missed days are empty, today is
  outlined. Opens over the home screen and closes via the X in its header.
  Backed by a new GET /api/history?start=&end= endpoint; no schema change
  (reads the existing completions table).

## 1.1.1 - 2026-07-18

- Fixed the burger menu overlapping the iOS status bar (battery/wifi). It
  now sits in the home header row, level with the "Today's Routine" title,
  instead of being absolutely positioned at the top of the screen.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-07-14

- Added a menu (burger icon, top-right of the home screen) as extensible
  chrome for app-wide actions, starting with a single "Level down" item.
- Added "Level down": decrements the current level by one (floor of 1).
  Completions logged at the old level are retagged to the new level so
  completion counts are preserved; "days at this level" restarts from
  today.
- Level down requires a confirm tap in the menu before it takes effect.

## 1.0.0 - (initial release)

- Initial release: daily bodyweight exercise routine tracker with guided
  session timers and manual level-up progression (levels 1-12).
